Your Onboarding Steps

Complete each item as soon as it becomes available to you.

1
Step 1 · Start here
Complete Your Onboarding Intake Form

Before anything else, please fill out the NY Connection onboarding intake form. The information you provide is used to start your background check, payroll setup, and system accounts.

Open the Onboarding Intake Form
2
Step 2
Complete Your Background Check

If you have not already completed your background check, please check the personal email address you provided during the hiring process.

Your background check invitation will be sent separately. Please also check your Spam/Junk folder if you do not see it.

Complete this as soon as possible, as your background check may need to be completed before additional onboarding steps can move forward.
3
Step 3
Complete Your ADP Enrollment

You will receive an invitation to enroll in ADP, our payroll and employee information system. As part of your ADP onboarding, you will need to complete:

●Form I-9
●Form W-4
●Direct Deposit Setup
●Any additional required employment documents

Please make sure all information is entered accurately and complete all assigned ADP tasks promptly.
4
Step 4
Review & Acknowledge the Employee Handbook

You will receive access to the NY Connection Employee Handbook during onboarding. Please review the handbook carefully and complete the required acknowledgment.

The handbook contains important information regarding company policies, attendance, workplace conduct, employee expectations, procedures, and other important information about working with NY Connection.
5
Step 5
Verizon & Wireless Zone Credentials

Several accounts and systems will be created or assigned to you throughout the onboarding process. These may include:

Wireless Zone Email
Your company email will generally follow the format FirstName.LastName@wireless-zone.com
RQ
Our point-of-sale system. Your Wireless Zone email will be used as your username.
Omni
Verizon’s system used for wireless activations and account management.
Optix
Verizon’s system used for Fios transactions and services.
Important: These credentials may not arrive at the same time. Some accounts cannot be created until previous onboarding steps have been completed and processed.

Please continuously monitor the personal email address you provided during onboarding, including your Spam/Junk folder. Some Verizon registration emails are time-sensitive, so complete any account registration or activation instructions as soon as you receive them.
6
Step 6
Complete Your Required P1 Trainings

Once your Verizon credentials are available, you will be required to complete your assigned P1 trainings.

These trainings are an important part of becoming fully operational and must be completed before you can independently perform certain Verizon transactions.

Access Verizon Training / Myworkday Portal

Save it as a favorite so you can do your readings from any device.

Your manager will provide guidance regarding your required courses and training expectations. Please work on your assigned training during your scheduled training time and complete all required courses promptly.
7
Step 7 · If applicable
Fios Registration

Employees working at locations that offer Verizon Fios may need additional registration, credentials, and/or training. This process may not be available until your initial Verizon credentials have been established.

If Fios access is required for your location, your manager will provide instructions on completing your registration and obtaining access to Optix.
Credential & Access Timeline
You may not get every system on day one

Your accounts are created through multiple systems, and some credentials depend on earlier onboarding steps being completed first. A typical onboarding progression looks like:

Intake Form → Background Check → ADP Enrollment → Employee Handbook → Verizon / Wireless Zone Credentials → P1 Training → RQ / Omni / Optix Access → Fios Registration, if applicable

Your manager and our support team will work with you throughout this process.

If you receive an account invitation or registration email, please complete it promptly. Delaying a registration step can delay the next part of your onboarding.
Your First Week
During your first several days, focus on:
✓Submitting your onboarding intake form
✓Completing all employment documentation
✓Setting up ADP and Direct Deposit
✓Reviewing the Employee Handbook
✓Activating your Wireless Zone/Verizon credentials
✓Beginning and completing your P1 training
✓Working with your manager to confirm your system access

Once you successfully gain access to your Wireless Zone email and Verizon credentials, please notify your manager so they can confirm your remaining accounts and access are being set up correctly.
