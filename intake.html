Onboarding Intake

Step 1 of your NY Connection onboarding.

Before you start, have these ready

  • Two different forms of ID, as photos or PDFs. A passport on its own is enough.
  • Your Social Security number and date of birth
  • Your home address, store location, and hiring manager's name
  • Your start date and shirt size for your name badge and uniform
You'll need a Google account. Google requires you to sign in to upload your ID documents. Any free Gmail account works.
Start Intake Form

Your information goes directly to NY Connection management and is used only for employee onboarding, payroll, and required employment verification. Questions about your onboarding should be directed to your hiring manager.
